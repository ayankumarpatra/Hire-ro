What this build does

Local processing

PDF text and candidate information are processed locally. No Gemini/API call is used in this final build.

Explainable score

The profile-fit percentage uses job-description similarity, skill coverage and basic resume evidence. It is a screening aid, not a hiring prediction.

Human review

A score does not verify a candidate's claims or prove that a missing mention means a skill is absent.
